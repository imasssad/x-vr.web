# Editing the site

Everything on tradeskillapps.com — every heading, paragraph, picture and
video — can be changed without touching code.

**Go to: [tradeskillapps.com/admin](https://tradeskillapps.com/admin)**

Sign in with GitHub. Your changes go live on their own about a minute after
you press Publish.

---

## How it works

The page is one long scroll, and the editor splits it into nine sections in
the order they appear:

| Section in the editor | Where it is on the page |
| --- | --- |
| Site basics, menu and footer | The top menu, the browser tab title, the footer |
| Hero | The big opening screen |
| Why this exists, mission, closing | The three points, the mission band, the closing band |
| Trades | The six trade cards |
| Our approach and the learning cycle | The "how a lesson works" section |
| Videos | The three film slots |
| Career pathways and levels | The four stages, and the schools/colleges cards |
| For educators and access | The educator steps and the equipment section |
| Glossary and FAQ | The terms list and the questions |

Pick a section, change what you need, press **Publish**.

---

## The things worth knowing

**Headlines come in two halves.** Most sections have a *Headline* and a
*Headline — highlighted part*. The first is dark navy; the second appears
underneath in blue italics. Splitting a sentence between the two is how the
two-tone effect is made.

**Pictures.** Click the picture box, then **Upload** to bring in a new one.
A good size is about 1600 pixels wide. Every picture sits in a frame of a
fixed shape, so it will be cropped from the centre to fit — put the important
part of the image in the middle.

**Always fill in "Description for screen readers."** It is read aloud to
people who cannot see the picture, and search engines use it. Describe what is
happening: *"An apprentice welding a steel joint, sparks visible"* — not
*"welding photo"*.

**Clearing a picture is safe.** Empty the box and the section falls back to
a neutral placeholder rather than breaking.

**Links.** Anything starting with `#` jumps to a section of this same page
(`#trades`, `#contact`). A full address like `https://…` goes to another site.

## Adding a video

Open **Videos**. There are three slots, and the easiest way to fill one is
YouTube:

1. Upload the film to YouTube.
2. Copy the part of the address after `v=` — in
   `youtube.com/watch?v=**dQw4w9WgXcQ**` that is `dQw4w9WgXcQ`.
3. Paste it into **YouTube video id**. Leave the two boxes below empty.
4. Publish.

The play button comes alive on its own. Until a slot has either a YouTube id
or a file, it shows a greyed-out "Film to be added" marker, which is there on
purpose — better than an empty gap.

You can add more than three videos, or remove one, with the **Add** and
**Delete** controls on the list.

## What not to change without asking

A few fields carry meaning beyond their text:

- **Short id** on a trade card is part of the web address. Changing it breaks
  any link anyone has already shared.
- **Responsive set** under the hero and approach pictures is generated. If you
  replace one of those two pictures, clear that box; otherwise leave it alone.
- **Icon** is a fixed list. The six trade icons and the four cycle icons are
  drawn to match — they cannot be uploaded.

---

## If something goes wrong

Every save is recorded. Nothing is ever truly lost: ask whoever maintains the
site and any earlier version can be restored exactly.

If a change doesn't appear after a few minutes, it is almost always the
deployment still running rather than the change not having saved.

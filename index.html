Illustrative concept

Welding

Heat, angle and travel speed decide the strength of a joint. Virtual practice builds the reasoning before a learner strikes an arc in the shop.

Foundations

  • Shop safety and personal protective equipment
  • Joint types, positions and common terminology
  • How heat, angle and travel speed shape a bead
  • Reading a finished weld for consistency

Sample immersive experience

  • Learn the concept — what makes a sound joint
  • Explore the environment — locate equipment and set up the work area
  • Explain the reasoning — describe each setup choice
  • Apply and demonstrate — practise with an instructor
Illustrative concept

HVAC

Air moves through a system in a sequence. Once a learner can trace that path and name each component, the equipment in front of them starts to make sense.

Foundations

  • How air moves through a system
  • Locating components and tracing airflow
  • The purpose each component serves
  • Identifying components on real equipment

Sample immersive experience

  • Learn the concept — how air moves through a system
  • Explore the environment — locate components, trace airflow
  • Explain the reasoning — describe each component's purpose
  • Apply and demonstrate — identify components on real equipment with an instructor
Illustrative concept

Electrical

A circuit is a path with a purpose. Learners follow that path, name what sits along it, and explain what each device is there to do.

Foundations

  • Circuit fundamentals and shared vocabulary
  • Conductors, devices and enclosures
  • Reading a simple diagram
  • Safe practice and verification habits

Sample immersive experience

  • Learn the concept — how a branch circuit is organised
  • Explore the environment — trace a circuit end to end
  • Explain the reasoning — describe what each device does
  • Apply and demonstrate — work through it with an instructor
Illustrative concept

Manufacturing

Parts move through a sequence, and every station has a reason to exist. Seeing the whole line makes a single operation easier to understand.

Foundations

  • Machine and work-cell layout
  • Measurement and tolerance vocabulary
  • Process sequence and quality checks
  • Safety zones and material handling

Sample immersive experience

  • Learn the concept — how a part moves through a process
  • Explore the environment — walk the cell and find each station
  • Explain the reasoning — describe why the order matters
  • Apply and demonstrate — follow the sequence with an instructor
Illustrative concept

Construction

Work on a site happens in order, and the plan explains that order. Learners practise reading it before they build from it.

Foundations

  • Site layout and the sequence of work
  • Materials, fasteners and assemblies
  • Reading plans and dimensions
  • Site safety and crew coordination

Sample immersive experience

  • Learn the concept — how an assembly comes together
  • Explore the environment — move through the space and locate the work
  • Explain the reasoning — describe the order of operations
  • Apply and demonstrate — build it under supervision
Illustrative concept

Emerging Technology

Sensors, controls and automation are joining established trades. Understanding how they fit makes a learner more useful on day one.

Foundations

  • Sensors, controls and automation vocabulary
  • How equipment data informs maintenance
  • Robotics and system interfaces
  • How new tools join established trades

Sample immersive experience

  • Learn the concept — what an automated cell is doing
  • Explore the environment — inspect the cell and its sensors
  • Explain the reasoning — describe how the parts communicate
  • Apply and demonstrate — review real equipment with an instructor

Trade terminology

The language of the work.

Skilled trades
Occupations built on specialized practical skills — welding, electrical, HVAC, carpentry and masonry among them.
Skilled labor
The broader category of workers with specialized training and knowledge. The trades are one part of it.
Craftsmanship / craft
The care, judgment, precision and practiced ability behind quality work.
Vocational or technical careers
Applied education routes — technical and trade schools, apprenticeships and employer programs. These can overlap with college.
